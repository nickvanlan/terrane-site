Local records

New tools. Deep local records.

Terrane Geomatics holds the survey records of Jones & Snyder Surveying (1976–2015): maps, field notes and monument history across the Sierra foothills. Those records help us find evidence faster and retrace lines with confidence. Every new survey is our own work, under our own contract.

Since 2015

Terrane Geomatics, Inc. has operated in Oakhurst under new ownership since 2015, doing business locally as Jones Snyder & Associates.
