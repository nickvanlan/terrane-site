Recent work

Selected projects.

Yosemite National Park · Laser scanning

Happy Isles & Vernal Fall

Topographic survey from the top of Vernal Fall down to Lady Franklin Rock, through Happy Isles and along the road back to Curry Village, captured with a 3D laser scanner.

Mariposa County · Underground

Blue Moon Mine decline

Boundary survey and mapping on BLM land, then line and grade and routine as-builts underground. The decline is more than half a mile long, within 5 feet of design along its length, and entirely on federal land.

Heavy civil · Yosemite

El Portal & Tuolumne Meadows treatment plants

Control and construction layout for a national contractor on two Yosemite wastewater plants, including structural steel and precast buildings, plus deformation monitoring with a 0.005 ft (1/16″) reporting threshold.

El Dorado County · Boundary & drone

Loon Lake

Forty remote acres in the high Sierra, reached by boat. We recovered the original section corners, solved the boundary, flew a drone topo with a 1-inch orthophoto, set corners and filed a record of survey.

Catalina Island to the Eastern Sierra

Mountaintop communication sites

Boundary, topography and records of survey for utility communication sites on peaks from Dakin Peak on Catalina Island to Bishop and Mammoth, for a long-time engineering client.

Agricultural · ALTA/NSPS

Large-acreage ALTAs

ALTA surveys and records of survey on ranches and farms of 1,000 to 5,000+ acres for institutional agricultural investors, across California.

Yosemite National Park · Design & layout

Bridalveil Fall accessible trails

All of the topographic surveying, mapping and construction layout for the Bridalveil Fall trails and overlooks.

Monterey County · Boundary

Big Sur coast

Boundary survey and record of survey on steep coastal property in Big Sur, with control from long static GNSS sessions where the terrain leaves little else.

Boundary · Retracement

1,000-acre ranch, original corners

Original GLO section corners recovered across a thousand acres, then intermediate points set along the property lines to guide new fencing.

Yosemite National Park · Federal boundary

Hazel Green

Boundary work for a National Park Service land exchange along Big Oak Flat Road, retracing federal section subdivisions to BLM Manual standards across two counties.
